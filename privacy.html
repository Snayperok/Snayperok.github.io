Privacy Policy

Podviyne Dno Publisher · Effective date: 28 October 2026

Who we are

Podviyne Dno Publisher (“the app”) is a private desktop tool operated by the owner of the video channel “Подвійне дно” (Podviyne Dno). It is used only by the channel owner to publish the channel’s own videos to the channel’s own accounts on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Contact: podviynednousa@gmail.com.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (Google): with the owner’s consent the app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload (to upload the channel’s own videos with their titles, descriptions, tags and scheduled publication time) and youtube.readonly (to read the channel name and confirm that an uploaded video was published).
  • TikTok: with the owner’s consent the app uses user.info.basic (account name), video.upload and video.publish (to post the channel’s own videos).
  • Instagram (Meta): with the owner’s consent the app uses basic account information and content publishing permissions to post the channel’s own Reels.

The app does not access, collect or process data of any other users, viewers or third parties.

How data is stored and used

Access tokens issued by the platforms are stored only locally, on the channel owner’s own computer. They are used solely to publish the owner’s videos and to check the publication status. No data is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any models. The app has no servers and no analytics.

Podviyne Dno Publisher’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

YouTube

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google’s handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the app. Access can be revoked at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/permissions, for TikTok in the TikTok app settings (Security → Manage app permissions), for Instagram in Settings → Apps and websites. Revoking access or deleting the app’s local folder removes all stored tokens.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: podviynednousa@gmail.com.
