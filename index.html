Podviyne Dno Publisher

Podviyne Dno Publisher is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of this channel. It uploads the channel’s own finished videos, with their titles and descriptions, to the channel’s own YouTube, TikTok and Instagram accounts on a schedule. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else’s data.
